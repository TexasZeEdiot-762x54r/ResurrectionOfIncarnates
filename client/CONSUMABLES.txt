Recovered consumable inventory

12 distinct types have original names/descriptions in text/common_text:
  LP Boost: 1, 3, 7, 14, 30 days (quotes 460484..460488 / 460501..460505)
  EXP Boost: 1, 3, 7, 14, 30 days (460489..460493 / 460506..460510)
  Tournament Ticket (460494 / 460511)
  Team Name Ticket (460496 / 460513)
The pack-of-five entries are shop bundles of the same ticket, not additional
consumable types. Other named event tickets do not establish active supported
consumable inventory entries; this is not proof of a complete live catalogue.

Original server IDs were not recovered. roi_consumables.py assigns explicit
revival identifiers using the client's decimal descriptor format, inspected
at 0x1400fdc60. These identifiers are not authentic original database IDs.
Names and descriptions use the original common_text quotes without rewriting
any localization resource. All twelve thumbnails now resolve to shipped
Other/Store_Thumbnail_Other_* textures: LP_001..005, Exp_001..005,
Tournament_001 and Teamname_001 (capitalization is significant).
The ten boost images explicitly show 1/3/7/14/30 days. Ticket _002 images
are bundles rather than single tickets.

Freeze correction: the native detail panel at 0x140339060 synchronously
loads the selected thumbnail. An empty list produces the bare Thumbnail/
directory and can strand the resource wait at 0x1407bc9a0. Captured on the
live frozen client, not inferred solely from an absent picture. The original
archive paths are verified by hash and texture decoding; no exe change or
inventory deletion is needed. Activation remains unimplemented as below.

Owned rows use sv_itemHoldId, sv_itemId and sv_itemNum (numeric strings),
as parsed at 0x1400fa555..0x1400fa84a. Stock is persisted in consumableItems
inside the local player profile. No inventory is automatically granted to
other accounts. Master rows alone do not imply ownership.

This change restores inventory/display, NOT activation, tournament entry,
team naming, original award rates or boost reward multiplication. useItem
remains unimplemented; do not assume the Use button activates a boost.
Adding inventory never activates timers, awards XP/LP, changes currency,
or grants grid/character unlocks. Existing no-invented-awards policy remains.
