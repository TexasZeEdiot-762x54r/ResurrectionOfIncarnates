Utsutahime 560 and Red Dragon 500 were supplied by the user on 2026-10-19,
not independently verified from footage. The old build's 1,000 HP for those
characters was readiness-only test data and has NOT been copied. All 14
released characters now have explicit HP; unreleased rows remain unchanged.

Only base HP changes. Stock costs, team stocks, skills, models, skeletons,
animation, inputs, damage handling and account data are unchanged. Normal
native spawning/respawning consumes this master data; no recurring live-health
override is installed. Actual maximum HP may also reflect equipped bonuses.

Every player should update their client package and restart the game to reload
the cached master table. No shared lobby-server restart or executable rebuild
is required for an existing server-backed installation.
