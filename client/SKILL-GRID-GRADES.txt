SKILL GRID SUMMARY GRADES - 2026-10-19

Cause and correction
  The original cube table has three positional effect entries. The executable
  parses entries 0 and 1 as combat effects, but entry 2 as summary category and
  score endpoints. Our old export removed zero-ID entries and shifted summary
  data into a combat slot. This left all eight summary categories at zero and
  could supply a spurious second combat effect. The old "three-effect cubes
  overflow two slots" conclusion was incorrect: the third slot has its own arm.

  All original slot positions now survive, including the empty second combat
  entry on ordinary cubes. Six formerly excluded dual-combat-effect cubes are
  appended after the original 340 entries, preserving every existing hold ID.
  All 346 released rows retain their shipped values. Disabled rows remain out.

Original native behavior
  Parser: 0x1400a2870; positional branch at 0x1400a32dc.
  Master summary category: byte +0x94; score min/max: floats +0x98/+0x9c.
  Parser scales source endpoints by 0.1, including summary scores.
  Selected-grid aggregation: 0x140349900. Only equipped, usable cubes contribute.
  Cube score interpolation: 0x1404db240, using native rank/level buckets.
  Summary display: 0x14034b900. Zero is '-'; positive scores below 20 are D;
  20..39 C, 40..59 B, 60..79 A, 80+ S. These are rating points, not percentages.
  Ordinary level-one ranged-damage I has 2% starting combat damage and 10
  starting attack-category rating points. The rating is not a 10% damage bonus.
  The native UI retains responsibility for recalculation, switching and removal.

Native removal/save compatibility
  An in-game removal test exposed a separate pre-existing validation error:
  remaining original cubes can serialize the same zero cell tag on every block
  (for example, 200 and 200 for hold 2). Those tags are not unique part IDs.
  The validator now accepts this observed all-zero encoding, with complete cube
  block counts, ownership, hold-list consistency and locked cells still checked.
  It does not allow partial cubes, extra blocks or new/unowned loadouts. Mixed
  nonzero legacy tags retain their uniqueness check pending further evidence.

Upgrade an existing recent client
  Close the game, overlay the complete client patch ZIP in the patch root,
  replacing included files, then launch normally. No Setup, executable rebuild,
  asset copying, profile migration or public-lobby restart is needed for this fix.
  Keep your own config and profile. Both players should update their own clients.

Scope
  No account levels, currencies, saved placements, unlock rights, XP policy or
  synthesis rules are changed. This restores shipped catalogue interpretation,
  not proof of final live-service balance or every combat-effect implementation.
  Automated source/layout tests do not replace an in-game visual check.
